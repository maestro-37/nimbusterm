NimbusTerm

A native terminal workspace for macOS.

SSH, SFTP, tunnels, keys, server monitoring, X11 forwarding and external RDP/VNC — in one app.

  • Installs into /Applications.
  • Passwords and passphrases live in macOS Keychain.
  • Requires macOS 14+ on Apple Silicon.

Developer: maestro37. If Gatekeeper warns about an unidentified developer, right-click NimbusTerm → Open.
